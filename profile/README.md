## What Is Valheim?

Valheim is a survival and exploration game developed by Iron Gate AB and published by Coffee Stain Publishing. Inspired by Norse mythology and Viking culture, it places players in a vast fantasy realm filled with mysterious landscapes, ancient ruins, dangerous creatures, and powerful bosses.

Players begin with limited equipment and must gather materials, craft tools, prepare food, and build shelter. As they explore, they discover new resources and crafting recipes that unlock better equipment, stronger structures, and additional ways to travel.

The world is procedurally generated, creating different landscapes and discoveries for each adventure. Dense forests, wetlands, snowy mountains, open plains, and other environments introduce unique resources, enemies, and survival challenges.

Valheim combines exploration with a detailed building system. Players can create simple shelters, large Viking halls, fortified settlements, farms, workshops, and coastal bases. Structural stability, comfort, and defense all contribute to a settlement's usefulness.

Combat and preparation are central to the experience. Players face hostile creatures, learn enemy attack patterns, manage health and stamina, and prepare suitable food and equipment before challenging powerful regional bosses.

The game supports solo adventures and cooperative play for groups of up to ten players. Friends can explore distant lands, build settlements, sail between regions, and face difficult encounters together.

## Key Features

### Procedurally Generated World

Explore a large fantasy realm shaped by Norse mythology. Different biomes contain distinct environments, resources, ruins, creatures, and secrets, giving players reasons to travel beyond their starting location.

### Viking Building System

Construct shelters, longhouses, workshops, defensive walls, and large settlements. A range of floors, walls, roofs, furniture, and decorative elements lets players create buildings suited to different environments and needs.

### Crafting and Resource Gathering

Collect wood, stone, metals, plants, and other materials to craft tools, equipment, furniture, and building components. New discoveries unlock additional recipes and crafting stations.

### Survival and Food Preparation

Prepare meals using gathered ingredients, farmed crops, and other resources. Different foods affect health and stamina, making meal selection an important part of preparation for exploration and combat.

### Sailing and Exploration

Build boats and travel across rivers and open seas to discover distant shores. Sailing connects separated regions and allows players to transport resources while searching for new places to explore.

### Boss Encounters

Each major biome features a powerful boss known as a Forsaken. Defeating these enemies unlocks important rewards and abilities that help players tackle subsequent regions.

### Solo and Cooperative Play

Play alone or invite friends to share a world. Cooperative adventures support shared building, resource gathering, exploration, and challenging encounters.

## Biomes and Exploration

### Meadows

The Meadows offer a relatively welcoming starting environment with useful resources, open spaces, and suitable locations for an initial settlement.

### Black Forest

Dense woodland introduces new materials, hostile creatures, caves, and additional crafting opportunities. Exploring this biome helps players prepare for tougher regions.

### Swamp

Wetlands contain dangerous enemies, crypts, and valuable materials. Careful preparation and suitable equipment are important when exploring this environment.

### Mountains

Cold conditions and powerful creatures make mountain travel demanding. Players need suitable protection, supplies, and equipment before venturing into snowy terrain.

### Plains

Open grasslands contain valuable resources and formidable enemies. Strong preparation helps players handle the threats found across this biome.

### Mistlands and Ashlands

These challenging regions introduce distinctive environments, dangerous creatures, and additional crafting materials. Players must adapt their equipment and tactics to the conditions found in each area.

### Deep North

The northern reaches form another part of Valheim's wider world. Available content and gameplay details depend on the current game build.

## Building and Settlement Management

### Base Construction

Create a small shelter or design a large fortified settlement. The construction system includes structural support requirements, allowing players to plan buildings that remain stable as they grow taller or expand.

### Crafting Stations

Workbenches, smelters, kilns, and other stations support the creation and refinement of materials. Placing useful stations within a well-organized base makes crafting and equipment management more convenient.

### Farming and Animal Taming

Cultivate crops, gather ingredients, and maintain suitable animals. These activities support food preparation and resource management during longer adventures.

### Comfort and Defense

Furniture, lighting, and interior decorations contribute to settlement comfort. Walls and other defensive structures can also help protect a base from hostile encounters.

## Combat and Character Preparation

### Equipment Selection

Craft and upgrade suitable equipment before entering dangerous areas. Different encounters reward careful preparation and an understanding of the character's available tools and protective gear.

### Health and Stamina

Combat relies on movement, blocking, dodging, and managing stamina. Running out of stamina at the wrong moment can make an encounter more difficult, so timing and positioning matter.

### Food and Rest

Meals affect the character's available health and stamina, while resting and maintaining comfort help prepare for the next journey.

### Boss Rewards

Defeating regional bosses grants rewards that unlock additional crafting options and abilities. These rewards help players prepare for the challenges found in later biomes.

## Multiplayer and World Management

Valheim supports both solo play and shared adventures. Players can host worlds, invite friends, construct shared settlements, and divide responsibilities during exploration.

Cooperative play can make demanding activities easier by allowing players to gather materials, transport supplies, construct bases, and prepare for major encounters together.

World settings allow players to adjust aspects of their experience. Available options depend on the current game build and selected world configuration.
